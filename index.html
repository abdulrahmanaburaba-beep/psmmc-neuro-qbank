About the questions

The Part 1, Part 2 and Promotion papers here are written to resemble the real examinations in style, level and blueprint. They are not the SCFHS question bank and nothing in them is copied from it. What you get is the common, important, general neurology a resident is expected to know — compiled from multiple neurology references and cross-checked against standard textbooks and current guidelines, with each explanation citing the source it rests on. Where published sources disagree, a question is marked check this one rather than presented as settled.
